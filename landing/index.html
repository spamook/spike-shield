What Spike Shield is

A waiting room for small apps built with AI tools. It sits between your visitors and your database, and only lets in as many people as the database can handle.

The script

One line in your app

A tiny script that loads before your app does. It asks our service whether this visitor can come in, and either starts the app or shows a waiting page. No backend changes, no SDK, no config in Supabase.

The queue

A counter that lives outside your stack

Our service counts who is active in your app right now. Above your threshold, new visitors wait in line and get in automatically as slots free up. It keeps counting while your database is having its worst day.

The dashboard

See the buzz as it happens

Active visitors, the length of the line, the emails people left while waiting, and an alert the moment a spike starts. Turn the Shield on or off and set the threshold from one page.

Built for people who ship an app in an afternoon with an AI coding tool, put it on Supabase, and post it somewhere. Not for engineers with a load balancer and an on-call rotation; they already have tools for this.

The problem

A post takes off, thousands of people open your app in an hour, and one of two things happens.

Downtime

The database falls over

Your app is static files on a CDN talking straight to Supabase. The CDN never breaks under load. Supabase does, usually on the one query the feed makes 200 times a second. The people the buzz brought in see an error and leave.

The bill

Usage pricing explodes

Every visit calls the database, and often an AI model too. Ten thousand visitors is ten thousand AI calls. The invoice arrives after the traffic is gone.

The fix

Surviving beats predicting

Knowing your app breaks at 80 users does not help at 2 a.m. when 800 show up. A queue in front of the app does. Everyone gets in, a few of them a minute later.

Install

One prompt in your AI coding tool.

The prompt makes two changes to your app. That is the whole install.

1

A script tag in index.html, first in <head>, so it runs before your bundle.

2

In src/main.tsx, the app waits for window.SpikeShield?.ready before it renders. Without the script that promise resolves at once.

Works with any static frontend that talks to Supabase from the browser: React with Vite or Parcel, whatever your tool ships.

Paste into your AI coding tool
Add Spike Shield to this app. In index.html, inside <head> and before any other script, add <script src="https://cdn.spikeshield.app/shield.js" data-site="YOUR-SITE-ID" data-api="https://api.spikeshield.app"></script> without async or defer. In src/main.tsx, wait for window.SpikeShield?.ready (use Promise.resolve() if it is undefined) before calling createRoot().render.
